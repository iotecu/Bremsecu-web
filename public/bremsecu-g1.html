Bremsecu G1

Guided Heavy-Vehicle Electrical Diagnostics

A rugged measurement platform for verifying standardized trailer and truck electrical interfaces with real values, controlled test sequences and documented evidence.

Purpose-built for the physical layer

Measure first. Verify second. Decide with evidence.

G1 focuses on the electrical conditions behind the fault: supply, ground integrity, signal state, wiring continuity, load behavior and CAN electrical conditions.

ISO 7638

ABS / EBS Interface

Direct voltage, ground and CAN electrical verification on the standardized trailer braking connection.

ISO 12098

15-Pin Electrical Interface

Structured pin-level checks for lighting and control circuits, including functional output verification.

CABLE

Cross-Scan Testing

Controlled continuity and cross-connection testing turns a long manual wiring task into a repeatable sequence.

LOAD

Lamp & Load Testing

Functional testing verifies that a circuit does more than show voltage — it checks behavior under controlled load conditions.

CAN

Electrical CAN Verification

CAN-H, CAN-L and safe de-energized termination checks add physical-layer evidence to communication diagnosis.

REPORT

Traceable Results

Measurements and findings are converted into a repeatable diagnostic record rather than remaining as undocumented workshop observations.

Guided workflow

Complex checks become a defined diagnostic sequence.

G1 guides the technician through connection selection, measurement, cable verification, functional checks, interpretation and reporting. The workflow reduces skipped steps and helps standardize how the same fault is approached by different technicians.
