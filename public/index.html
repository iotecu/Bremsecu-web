What Bremsecu does

Hardware, software and methodology in one diagnostic workflow.

Bremsecu combines direct electrical measurement with guided digital workflows to help technicians verify the physical layer of heavy-vehicle systems.

01

Vehicle-Independent Diagnostics

A structured approach designed around electrical reality rather than a single vehicle brand or OEM software path.

02

Pin-Level Verification

Measure supply, ground and signal conditions directly at the relevant connection points.

03

Guided Workflow

The application turns complex checks into a repeatable sequence that supports clearer decisions in the workshop.

04

Cable Cross-Scan

Systematically inspect wiring continuity and cross-connections using a controlled test process.

05

Functional Testing

Evaluate critical electrical functions through dedicated test paths for trailer and truck-side systems.

06

Evidence-Based Reporting

Turn measurements and test results into traceable diagnostic evidence instead of undocumented trial and error.
