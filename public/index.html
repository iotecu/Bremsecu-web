Measure the electrical reality behind the fault.

Bremsecu combines real electrical measurement with guided verification, comparison logic and diagnostic evidence. G1 applies the methodology to heavy-vehicle standardized interfaces; WT Pro extends it to pin-level measurement, dynamic signals and trainable connector knowledge.
