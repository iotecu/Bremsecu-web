Measurement Methodology

Bremsecu is not only a diagnostic suitcase. It is the applied form of a patented heavy-vehicle measurement methodology built around direct verification, guided testing, correlated results and documented evidence.

Why methodology matters

Fault codes are useful. Electrical reality is decisive.

Heavy-vehicle electrical faults can remain unclear when diagnosis depends only on reported errors, software interpretation or technician intuition. Bremsecu adds a repeatable physical verification layer and turns a complex manual process into a guided diagnostic workflow.

01

Start with the physical layer

Verify supply, ground, signal and wiring conditions before drawing conclusions.

02

Follow a defined sequence

Guided steps reduce skipped checks and make the process easier to repeat across technicians.

03

Cross-check the result

Use cable and functional verification to confirm whether the measured condition is real and localizable.

04

Separate symptom from cause

The methodology is designed to move from an observed fault toward the electrical condition that explains it.

05

Remain vehicle-independent

The workflow is built around standardized connections and measurable electrical behavior rather than a single OEM ecosystem.

06

Create evidence

Document measurements and outcomes so the diagnosis can be reviewed, explained and repeated.

From wiring to system visibility

See the path, not just the symptom.

The methodology treats the vehicle electrical system as a measurable network: connectors, wiring paths, grounds, power feeds and control units all contribute to the final behavior seen by the technician.

Bremsecu provides the practical tools and guided workflow needed to inspect that network in a consistent way.

Designed for rapid adoption

Short training. Guided operation. Consistent results.

Bremsecu is designed so that a technician who is already comfortable using a smartphone can learn the operating flow in a very short time. The interface guides the user through the required steps, while the system manages measurement order, safety checks and result evaluation in the background.

01

Minimal learning curve

No complex software environment or specialist user interface is required. The workflow is designed around familiar mobile interaction.

02

Automatic guidance

The technician is led through the correct sequence step by step instead of having to remember pin assignments, test combinations and evaluation rules.

03

Built-in safety logic

Safety conditions and test prerequisites are checked by the system before sensitive test functions are allowed to proceed.

Correlated diagnostics

Bremsecu does not simply collect measurements. It correlates them.

Voltage, wiring continuity, cross-connections, current, ground, signal and CAN results are evaluated together rather than shown as isolated PASS/FAIL records.

The system uses those relationships to guide the technician toward the likely fault area, probable cause and recommended next verification step.

01Measurements are collected in a controlled sequence.
02Results are compared across related circuits and test stages.
03The user is guided toward the most relevant next check.
04The final outcome is converted into a clear diagnostic record.
From hours to minutes

A diagnostic process that can take 6–8 hours manually can be reduced to minutes.

Manual diagnosis may require repeated pin measurements, reference comparisons, continuity checks, cross-short testing and note-taking across many circuits. Bremsecu automates and structures those tasks so the technician can reach a usable result dramatically faster.

Bremsecu transforms expert technician know-how into a structured system of measurement order, safety rules, comparison logic, data recording and reporting. The value is not limited to performing many measurements; it lies in transferring expert diagnostic discipline into a repeatable workflow that can be used consistently across a service organization.

Detailed reporting

The diagnosis does not end with a PASS or FAIL.

Bremsecu converts measured data into a detailed diagnostic report that can include the tested functions, measured values, detected inconsistencies, likely fault area, probable cause and recommended next control step.

A patented technology designed to scale

Not limited to a specific connector, vehicle or machine group.

Bremsecu is more than a device that measures selected sockets. Its patented methodology is designed to measure electrical systems from both sides, verify results, correlate findings and guide the technician toward the correct next control step.

The same measurement, verification and guidance logic used for ISO 7638 and ISO 12098 can be adapted to other standardized vehicle and machine interfaces when their pin structure, normal electrical conditions, communication characteristics, load behavior and safety limits are defined.

This makes Bremsecu a unique wiring test and diagnostic technology with the potential to expand into additional heavy-vehicle, agricultural, construction and special-purpose applications.
