What is Bremsecu?

Bremsecu is an electrical diagnostic methodology and product family built around real measurement, reference values, guided verification and repeatable diagnostic evidence.

Designed for the physical layer

Measure first. Verify second. Decide with evidence.

Bremsecu focuses on the electrical conditions behind the fault: power supply, ground integrity, signal state, wiring continuity and functional behavior.

ISO 7638Direct measurement and verification on ABS/EBS trailer connections.
ISO 12098Structured checks for 15-pin heavy-vehicle electrical connections.
Cable TestControlled continuity and cross-connection scanning.
Lamp TestFunctional output verification with guided steps.
CAN TerminationGuided checks designed for safe, de-energized termination testing.
ReportingDocument test outcomes as repeatable diagnostic evidence.
